What we do

Hazard survey and agent selection

We survey the room, look at the fire load, and recommend whether a clean agent system is the right answer at all.

System design and installation

Cylinders, distribution pipework, nozzles, detection and the total flood arrangement, installed and handed over with documentation.

Servicing and cylinder management

Scheduled servicing, leak and pressure checks, and coordinating cylinder replacement when the agent level needs topping up.

Common questions

Clean agent systems are designed so that the agent concentration stays within safe exposure limits in the room, but the room must still be evacuated on alarm and the door released. We design the arrangement and brief your staff on it.

Often when the room holds ordinary combustibles, or where a sprinkler system is simpler and cheaper. A sprinkler system is usually the better answer for a warehouse or a workshop, so we will say so rather than sell you a system you do not need.

The cylinders are monitored, and we check them during servicing. When the agent level needs topping up we coordinate the replacement.
