Selection and safe use

Choose an extinguisher for the materials and risks present

Fire extinguishers are not interchangeable. The correct type depends on the materials that could burn, the location, equipment and the applicable site requirements. Water, foam, dry powder, carbon dioxide and wet chemical units have different intended uses and limitations; the label and manufacturer instructions should guide selection. Electrical equipment, cooking oils and reactive metals need particular care, so do not assume one extinguisher is suitable for every hazard.

Portable extinguishers are a first-response measure only where a person is trained, the fire is still small, the alarm has been raised and a safe exit remains behind them. Evacuate and call the emergency services if there is doubt, smoke is spreading, or the fire cannot be controlled immediately. Never delay evacuation to retrieve or operate an extinguisher.

Keep extinguishers visible, reachable and matched to their location. Arrange inspection and servicing in accordance with the equipment instructions and applicable requirements. Share the extinguisher label, type, quantity, condition and site location when asking about fire extinguisher supply, servicing or refill support.
