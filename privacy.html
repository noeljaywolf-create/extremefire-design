What this notice covers

This notice explains what personal information Extreme Fire Design Inc collects through this website, why we collect it, and what we do with it. It is written in plain language. If anything here is unclear, contact us and we will explain.

Who we are

Extreme Fire Design Inc is a fire protection engineering contractor based at 21566 Damofalls Industrial Area, Harare, Zimbabwe. We are the data controller for the information described here.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Your name, WhatsApp or phone number, email address, and the details you type into an enquiry form, such as your site location and the system you need.
  • Your name and contact details when you download a guide or ask for a compliance checklist.
  • Your answers to the fire safety compliance quiz.

Information collected automatically

  • Standard web server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages you visited.
  • Analytics information about how the site is used, so we can see which pages are useful. See "Analytics" below.
  • Marketing campaign parameters, such as utm_source and utm_campaign, which tell us which advert or link brought you to the site.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote or site survey.
  • To send you the guide or checklist you asked for.
  • To score your compliance quiz and send you the result.
  • To understand which parts of the website are useful, so we can improve them.
  • To meet our record-keeping and legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertising networks.

WhatsApp

If you contact us on WhatsApp, your messages, phone number and profile information are held by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy, and are also received by us. We use them only to answer your enquiry. Please do not send sensitive personal or financial information over WhatsApp.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses cookies and similar technologies. Analytics tools such as Google Analytics measure how visitors use the site. You can decline analytics cookies in your browser settings, or use a browser privacy mode, and the site will still work. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and contact records for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and to meet legal and contractual record-keeping requirements, and then delete or anonymise them. Marketing contact details are kept until you ask us to remove them.

Your rights

You can ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. You can also object to us using it to contact you for marketing. Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act sets out these rights. Email info@extremefire.co.zw to make a request.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please do not send passwords, bank details or identity documents through this website.
