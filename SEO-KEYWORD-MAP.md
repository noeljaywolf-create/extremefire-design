# SEO & AEO keyword map — Extreme Fire Design Inc

Planning document. It maps every target keyword to a page, a title, a meta
description and a search intent. It does not change any URL, canonical or the
Google Tag Manager container.

> **Constraints this map respects**
> - The site is live and indexed on Google. **No URL, canonical or redirect changes.**
> - Google Tag Manager container `GTM-KT2WWMKV` is installed on all pages and is
>   **not touched** by any keyword or copy change.
> - The site is **fact-gated**: no invented prices, statistics, client names or
>   credentials. Keywords are targeted through accurate service descriptions only.

**Important:** these are researched keyword *ideas*, not verified search-volume
rankings. Confirm actual demand in Google Keyword Planner and Search Console
before committing budget.

---

## 1. Strategy in one line

Target the words a buyer actually types when they need fire equipment or fire
safety work — **not only "fire protection"**. Sell and service fire
extinguishers for **every seat**: cars, homes, schools, offices, plus industry.

The site already has one page per system. This map adds the missing axis: the
**buyer segment** (who) crossed with the **transaction** (buy, service, refill,
inspect).

## 11. AEO (answer engine optimisation) notes

Answer engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) read
`llms.txt`, structured data and plain question-and-answer text.

- Keep **one question per FAQ entry**, answered in the first sentence.
- Keep `FAQPage` schema and the **visible** FAQ text identical.
- Keep `llms.txt` current with service scope, including the buyer segments.
- Prefer concrete, checkable statements over marketing adjectives.
